Privacy Policy

We treat your personal information like we treat our signature dishes—carefully, transparently, and respectfully. This page explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, make a reservation, or message us, we may collect the following:

  • Personal details such as your name, email, phone number, delivery address, and any notes you share with us.
  • Payment information shared through our secure checkout partners.
  • Automatically collected data like device identifiers, browser type, IP address, and browsing behavior to help us improve your experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders or inquiries efficiently.
  • Send you updates when you sign up for our newsletter, promotions, or loyalty alerts.
  • Analyze how our website and menu pages perform so we can continue to evolve.

Cookies, Tracking & Third Parties

Cookies help us remember your preferences and keep the site running smoothly. You can manage cookie settings via your browser, but disabling them may impact certain features. We may also share aggregated or anonymized data with trusted partners, such as payment processors and analytics providers.

Security & Your Choices

We follow industry-standard practices to protect your information. You can request updates, corrections, or removal by emailing hello@nammaillam.com. We respond within a reasonable timeframe.
