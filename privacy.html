Privacy Policy

We handle your information with the same warmth and respect that guides our kitchen. Here is a clear outline of how your data is collected, used, and protected.

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, book tables, place takeaway orders, or submit catering/franchise inquiries, we may collect the following details:

  • Contact Information: Your name, phone number, email address, and delivery or event location.
  • Inquiry Details: Specific culinary requests, guest counts, dietary notes, or franchise proposals.
  • Digital Usage Data: Technical logs including browser type, operating system, and IP address collected automatically to maintain website stability and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely to deliver genuine hospitality services, including:

  • Confirming table reservations and food takeaway orders.
  • Responding to catering requests and franchise applications.
  • Sharing occasional news, festive specials, or menu updates if you have opted in.
  • Improving website usability, layout responsiveness, and browsing speed.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement standard organizational and technical security measures to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing companies.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party services such as Google Maps for directions and WhatsApp for direct customer care messaging. These services operate under their own independent privacy terms.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You may at any time request an update, correction, or deletion of your contact records by emailing us at nammaillamofficial@gmail.com.

Have questions regarding your data?

Our privacy and customer care team is available to help anytime.
