FAQ's

Answers to the questions we hear the most about dining with us, ordering in, or celebrating at Namma Illam.

Do you take reservations?

Yes, reservations are available for in-house dining. Please call us directly or use the contact form on the website to hold a table.

Can I order ahead?

We accept advance orders over the phone and through our delivery partners. Mention any allergies or special requests and we’ll note them.

Do you cater events?

We provide off-site catering for small events. Email hello@nammaillam.com with your guest count and date, and we’ll follow up with a tailored menu.

What safety measures are in place?

Our kitchen follows strict hygiene protocols, from regular sanitization to staff health checks. Delivery partners follow contactless handoffs whenever possible.

Can I change or cancel my delivery?

Cancellations or changes are accepted before the order is confirmed. Contact us as soon as you can—we’ll do our best to accommodate if the food hasn’t been prepared yet.
