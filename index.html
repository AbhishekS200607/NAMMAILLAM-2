Tradition on a Plate,
Memories in
Every Bite.

In Malayalam, Namma Illam means 'Our Home' - the spirit at the heart of our kitchen. More than a vegetarian restaurant, we are a tribute to the warmth of a traditional South Indian home. Rooted in ancestral heritage, we serve authentic, slow-cooked flavors that honor history, hospitality, and soulful dining.

A Taste of Tradition

From steaming idlis and crispy dosas to filter coffee that warms your soul, our menu celebrates the best of South Indian cuisine. Every dish is crafted with fresh ingredients, traditional methods, and a dash of innovation.

A perfectly crisp dosa layered with ghee and spicy podi, wrapped around a flavorful potato masala. It’s the classic dosa you love, just more bold, aromatic, and satisfying.
